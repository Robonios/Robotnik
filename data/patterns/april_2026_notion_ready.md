# Robotnik Patterns — April 2026

**Three signals visible only in aggregate.**

April 2026 cleared $100.5B across 86 frontier-tech rounds. SpaceX's $75B IPO filing dominates the headline; strip it and the month still ran $25.5B in private capital, well above the $11–14B monthly base of late 2025. Sector mix tells more than the total: Space took $88.4B, Semiconductors $9.2B, Robotics $2.9B, Materials a thin $22M across two rounds. Three patterns are visible only in aggregate.

## I. The frontier-tech exit window has reopened

Twelve frontier-tech companies took IPO, M&A, or Pre-IPO actions in April. That's not a count we've seen since 2021. Aggregate exit-track value: $91B. SpaceX filed its S-1 at $1.75T. Amazon closed $11.6B for Globalstar. Cerebras refiled at $24B target valuation. DustPhotonics was absorbed into Credo (CRDO) at $1.3B. SJ Semiconductor listed in Shanghai at $690M. York Space rolled up ALL.SPACE for $355M. DeepWay marked a $310M Pre-IPO. Rocket Lab acquired Mynaric for $155M. Three additional M&A events closed at undisclosed sizes.

Two readings compete. Bearish: private capital is rotating out at the top tick. Bullish: this is migration — companies that priced as private unicorns are hitting public-market scale where strategic acquirers and index funds are the natural holders. Capital doesn't leave the sector; it reorganises.

We lean migration. Seven of twelve events involve a strategic acquirer (Credo, Amazon, Rocket Lab, York Space, Phantom Space, plus two undisclosed strategics on Polariton Technologies and Premium Robotics). That's consolidation logic — incumbents buying capability before the next cycle — not generalist rotation. The bear case has the easier proof: if Q3 2026 IPO pricing on SpaceX or Cerebras comes in soft, the migration thesis breaks immediately.

## II. Chinese humanoid raised 5× what US humanoid raised in April

Five Chinese humanoid + adjacent robotics companies raised $1.48B: TARS ($455M Pre-Series A), DeepWay ($310M Pre-IPO), Galaxea AI ($290M Series B extension at $2.9B post), X Square Robot ($276M Series B, Xiaomi + HongShan-led), Pudu Robotics ($150M at $1.5B post). US humanoid raised $0 in April. The nearest US comparables closed in March: Mind Robotics' $500M Series A at $2B (Andreessen Horowitz, Accel) and Sunday's $165M Series B at $1.15B (Coatue solo lead).

The TARS round size is the load-bearing data point. $455M for a Pre-Series A is round-size geometry we haven't seen in this category, in either geography. Valuation undisclosed, but the implied post-money — likely $2–3B — puts TARS into deployment-capital pricing two rounds earlier than its US peers, even allowing for Chinese state-aligned syndicates that compress the round-letter sequence.

This contradicts the consensus that US humanoid leads. Capital is voting otherwise. Public-market read-through: XPENG (XPEV) and BYD (2533 HK) are the closest listed proxies for the Chinese humanoid deployment thesis. Private marks like TARS at an implied $2–3B post set the floor for how those listed names' humanoid programs get repriced over the next 18–24 months.

## III. Silicon photonics merchant ecosystem is consolidating

DustPhotonics' $1.3B M&A into Credo is the loudest April datapoint for optical interconnect. Paired with Polariton Technologies' M&A the same week, and Ayar Labs' $500M Series E close in early March, the category is bifurcating fast. The merchant photonics independents — Lightmatter ($4.4B private), Ayar Labs (Intel-aligned), Celestial AI (AMD strategic-LP'd) — are being absorbed into chipco roadmaps or capital structures that look strategic, not financial. Lightmatter is the only remaining clean-merchant pure-play at multi-billion private scale.

Credo (CRDO) is a connectivity vendor whose active electrical cables compete with optical interconnect; absorbing DustPhotonics is a hedge against being outflanked by AI-fabric incumbents. The merchant silicon photonics ecosystem opened with the 2023 NVIDIA-Mellanox-adjacent wave; if April's pattern continues, the window may close as fast as it opened. The next round of value capture will accrue to chipcos with native optical interconnect (NVDA, MRVL, AVGO) and the foundries that fabricate them (TSM). Merchant photonics startups not acquired by year-end will face Series D pricing pressure as strategic exit optionality narrows.

## What to watch in May

Whether SpaceX or Cerebras prices in above or below pre-filing private marks. That's the migration-vs-exit binary, and the answer arrives within 60 days.
