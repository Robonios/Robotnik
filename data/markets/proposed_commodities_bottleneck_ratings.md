## CRITICAL (6)

These commodities meet all three CRITICAL anchors: single-country concentration at or above 70%, no commercial substitute today, and a multi-year qualification path. Removal would halt or severely degrade physical AI stack production within 1-2 quarters.

### Dysprosium
- **Bottleneck:** Sole-source heavy REE for high-temperature stabilization of NdFeB magnets in EV traction motors and robotic servos operating above 80C.
- **Concentration:** China approximately 99% of separated Dy (USGS 2024). Myanmar Kachin feedstock approximately 50% of Chinese imports.
- **Substitution status:** None at scale. Grain boundary diffusion reduces Dy loading but does not eliminate. Niron Magnetics iron nitride is pre-commercial — first plant late 2025/early 2026, CES 2026 EV/robotics prototypes only. Not qualified for high-temp traction.
- **Verifier verdict:** HOLD — three CRITICAL bars verified May 2026. Lynas Malaysia first commercial Dy oxide May 2025 represents under 5% of global supply; projected 15-20% by 2030. April 4, 2025 MOFCOM Announcement No. 18 remains operative (the November 2025 suspension applied only to the October 9, 2025 controls). IEA recorded European Dy/Tb prices at 6x Chinese domestic equivalent at peak.

### Samarium
- **Bottleneck:** SmCo magnet feedstock for >200C operating environments (defense radar, missile actuators, satellites, jet engines, high-temp industrial robotics) where NdFeB cannot reach.
- **Concentration:** China approximately 90-95% of separated Sm; >60% of output and near-total separation/refining infrastructure (USGS-aligned).
- **Substitution status:** None for >200C niche. NdFeB caps at ~150-200C even with heavy Dy loading; SmCo5 handles 300C, Sm2Co17 handles 550C.
- **Verifier verdict:** HOLD — April 2025 MOFCOM Announcement No. 18 explicitly added Sm to medium/heavy REE licensing controls; per-shipment licensing enforcement confirmed by CSIS/Taylor Wessing. Lynas Malaysia HREE first Sm oxide March 2026 is sub-commercial-scale; MP Materials Mountain Pass heavy REE separation targets mid-2026. Defense qualification 2-4 years beyond first production.

### Tungsten (APT + WF6 sub-field)
- **Bottleneck:** APT (ammonium paratungstate) refined intermediate and W metallization process gas for semiconductor contacts/plugs/3D NAND word lines.
- **Concentration:** China 82.7% mined W (USGS MCS 2025: 67,000t of 80,000t global); approximately 85% APT refining (ITIA).
- **Substitution status:** No commercial substitute for W plug/contact at advanced logic; Mo partial in some 3D NAND word lines (multi-year qualification); fluorine-free W precursors R&D-stage.
- **Verifier verdict:** HOLD — Feb 4, 2025 China MOFCOM Announcement No. 10 placed APT, W oxides, W carbides on dual-use licensing list. Binding enforcement confirmed: APT exports fell from 782t (2024) to 243t in first 11 months of 2025, ~70% YoY decline (Fastmarkets, chemradar). Almonty Sangdong (Korea) commercial ramp 2025 covers only 17-20% non-China share.

### Yttrium
- **Bottleneck:** YAG laser hosts (LiDAR, fiber laser cutting, defense), YSZ thermal barrier coatings (jet engine turbine blades), YBCO superconductors (fusion magnets, MRI), display phosphors.
- **Concentration:** China approximately 90% of global Y supply, ~97% of mining output, ~91% of REE refining capacity. US has 100% net import reliance, ~93% from China (USGS 2024/2025).
- **Substitution status:** No commercial drop-in for YAG laser hosts (Nd:YVO4, Nd:YLF themselves Y-bearing or alternative-gain materials), YSZ TBCs (rare-earth zirconates, hafnia-stabilized zirconia under multi-year aero qualification), or YBCO at performance parity.
- **Verifier verdict:** HOLD — April 4, 2025 MOFCOM Announcement No. 18 explicitly includes Y. Regime "never suspended" and Chinese customs data shows Y exports ~50% below pre-restriction baseline. Lynas Y recovery deferred to future expansion phase beyond April 2026 Sm milestone. Sits above Ga HIGH anchor due to lack of recyclate channel.

### Terbium
- **Bottleneck:** Heavy REE for high-coercivity high-temperature NdFeB stabilization in EV traction and humanoid robotics servo grades (N42SH/N48EH), plus green YAG/garnet phosphor activation.
- **Concentration:** China >99% separated Tb (USGS 2024). Myanmar Kachin feedstock to Chinese separation. Tb market thinner than Dy (~400-500 t/yr vs ~1,200 t/yr) — arguably worsens vulnerability.
- **Substitution status:** No commercial substitute for high-coercivity high-temp magnet stabilization. Grain boundary diffusion reduces but does not eliminate Tb loading.
- **Verifier verdict:** HOLD — MOFCOM Announcement 18 (April 4, 2025) explicitly added Tb to dual-use list alongside Sm/Gd/Dy/Lu/Sc/Y, building on December 3, 2024 heavy REE controls. Licenses reportedly delayed/denied for Western OEMs Q2 2025. Lynas Malaysia heavy REE circuit still ramping; Iluka Eneabba refinery delayed to 2026+. NdFeB recyclate for Tb recovery sub-5% globally.

### Antimony  (USER OVERRIDE — verifier proposed HIGH, founder retained CRITICAL)
- **Bottleneck:** Flame retardants (~50% of demand), lead-acid battery hardening, PV glass clarifier, and defense applications (armor-piercing, night vision, nuclear weapons).
- **Concentration:** China ~48% mining (USGS 2024); processing approximately 80% when including Russian/Tajik feed.
- **Substitution status:** Commercial substitutes exist (Tolsa ADINS, NZC-FLMSYN-03, FireBlend 33-A, Clariant Exolit OP phosphorus-based) but require multi-year requalification per application; defense and lead-acid battery roles have weaker substitution.
- **Verifier verdict:** DOWNGRADE to HIGH.
- **Founder override:** Hold at CRITICAL. The Aug 14, 2024 MOFCOM export controls are actively binding, Perpetua (US) does not reach commercial scale until 2028, and Mandalay (AU) is sub-scale. Same internal logic as Dy/Sm CRITICAL: current sole-source reality, not future alternatives, sets the rating. The Nov 10, 2025 suspension of the Dec 2024 prohibition reverts to licensing (not denial), which is still binding policy weaponization consistent with the CRITICAL anchor. Calibration-consistent at CRITICAL.

## HIGH (7)

These commodities meet two of three CRITICAL anchors but allow capex or substitution paths within reach. Removal forces production cuts and emergency requalification.

### Neodymium (NdPr oxide)
- **Bottleneck:** Workhorse light REE for NdFeB permanent magnets in servo motors, robotic actuators, EV traction motors, humanoid joints.
- **Concentration:** China 85-92% of separated/refined NdPr (USGS 2024/2025; industry consensus). Mining more diversified (US ~15%, Australia ~12%).
- **Substitution status:** No commercial substitute for high-coercivity NdFeB in compact high-torque applications; ferrite/SmCo sacrifice 4-5x energy density. Recycling <5% of supply.
- **Verifier verdict:** DOWNGRADE from CRITICAL to HIGH. April 2025 MOFCOM Announcement No. 18 deliberately EXCLUDED light REEs (Nd, Pr, La, Ce); only heavy/medium REEs covered. Lynas Malaysia LAMP 5,000-6,000 t/yr NdPr oxide and MP Materials 2,599t in 2025 (+101% YoY) provide commercially qualified non-China supply today. NdPr matches Gallium HIGH anchor pattern (high concentration + recyclate/diversification underway), not Dy CRITICAL anchor.

### Manganese (HPMSM)
- **Bottleneck:** High-purity manganese sulfate monohydrate (>99.9% purity for cathode use) — purification chokepoint distinct from raw Mn ore, which is multi-country.
- **Concentration:** China 90-95% of HPMSM production capacity (Benchmark Mineral Intelligence, CRU 2023-2024). Mn ore: South Africa ~36%, Gabon/Australia/Brazil multi-source.
- **Substitution status:** Substitution actually WORSENS bottleneck — rising LMR/LMFP/high-Mn NMC adoption (Tesla/Ford/GM) increases HPMSM demand. Mn recyclate path nascent.
- **Verifier verdict:** HOLD — Element 25 Louisiana DOE-backed plant ~2026, Euro Manganese Bécov ~2027, South32 Hermosa all pre-commercial. Cathode qualification adds 12-18 months. Matches Gallium HIGH anchor (slightly less acute on policy lever, stronger on demand tailwind). No binding export controls yet (watch item).

### Graphite (natural spherical + synthetic anode)
- **Bottleneck:** Anode active material for commodity Li-ion across all EV, grid, and robotics battery packs.
- **Concentration:** China 78% mined natural graphite (USGS MCS 2025: 1.27Mt of 1.63Mt 2024); >90% spherical and synthetic anode material (Benchmark, MMTA).
- **Substitution status:** Silicon and Si-C composite (Sila, Group14, Amprius) under 5% of anode market through 2026. No full substitute for graphite in commodity Li-ion.
- **Verifier verdict:** HOLD — China MOFCOM/GAC export controls effective Dec 1, 2023 are binding: Jan-Feb 2024 spherical graphite exports fell 64.5% YoY (7,240t to 2,567t per Fastmarkets), permit processing extended to 30-40 days. Sila Moses Lake targets only 10 GWh by 2026. Syrah Vidalia at 11.25ktpa AAM (45ktpa FID pending). Borderline-CRITICAL on strict three-bar reading; held at HIGH because controls are licensing not embargo and small ex-China commercial supply exists.

### Magnesium
- **Bottleneck:** Lightweighting (humanoid robot structural frames, EV components, aerospace) and Al-alloying. Concentrated in Shaanxi province.
- **Concentration:** China 85-87% of primary Mg (USGS 2024); approximately 50% from Shaanxi (Yulin/Fugu area).
- **Substitution status:** Al partial substitute (denser, lower stiffness-to-weight); polymer composites in niche applications. No full substitute for lightest-weight structures.
- **Verifier verdict:** HOLD — Sept-Oct 2021 Shaanxi power curtailment is documented real-world supply shock that threatened European auto production. No formal export controls yet, but 2021 crisis substitutes for export-control evidence by demonstrating real bottleneck. Ex-China capacity (US Magnesium Utah ~50kt, Latrobe, Magontec) small vs 1Mt+ global demand.

### Gallium (metal + GaAs sub-field)
- **Bottleneck:** Primary Ga for GaAs/GaN power amplifiers, RF, photonics. Reference anchor for HIGH.
- **Concentration:** China 98% of primary low-purity gallium (USGS 2024); 99% (MCS 2025).
- **Substitution status:** GaN-on-Si emerging but Ga-dependent; InP partial substitute for optoelectronics but itself has ~70% supply-demand gap in 2025. "Completely gallium-free solutions with comparable performance still in research stage."
- **Verifier verdict:** HOLD — MOFCOM Notice 2024 No. 46 (Dec 3, 2024) banned Ga dual-use exports to US; suspended Nov 9, 2025 until Nov 27, 2026, reverting to licensing; military end-user ban remains. CSIS: trade truce did NOT solve gallium problem. Kazakhstan ERG starts Q3 2026 at 15 MT/yr only. Proposal language mirrors HIGH reference anchor near-verbatim.

### Photoresist (EUV + ArF immersion)
- **Bottleneck:** EUV resist for leading-edge logic/memory at N3/N2/A14 nodes. ArF immersion resist for prior generations.
- **Concentration:** Japan ~90%+ EUV resist (JSR, TOK, Shin-Etsu, Sumitomo Chemical, Fujifilm); ~80% ArF immersion.
- **Substitution status:** No commercial non-Japan EUV resist qualified at HVM. DuPont/Dow hold ~15% ArF share and Dongjin Semichem (Korea) ArF qualification progressing — ArF substitution depth meaningfully greater than EUV.
- **Verifier verdict:** DOWNGRADE from CRITICAL to HIGH. Qualification timeline 18-24 months (not 5-10 years like Dy substitution science). **Jurisdictional risk is the load-bearing distinction:** Japan is treaty ally, integral to CHIPS Act-aligned coalition. The Dy CRITICAL anchor requires hostile-jurisdiction policy weaponization. JSR/JIC acquisition (2024) is strategic consolidation but in allied jurisdiction. Maps to Ga HIGH anchor; two-of-three CRITICAL anchors → HIGH per framework rule.

### Synthetic fused silica / EUV mask blanks
- **Bottleneck:** Mo/Si multilayer EUV mask blanks on ULE/Zerodur substrates — every leading-edge fab depends on this.
- **Concentration:** AGC + Hoya + Shin-Etsu ~100% of EUV mask blanks (all Japan). ULE substrate (Corning, US) sole-sourced upstream.
- **Substitution status:** No alternative chemistry at production scale. Multi-decade IP for defect-free multilayer deposition. Qualification 3-5+ years.
- **Verifier verdict:** DOWNGRADE from CRITICAL to HIGH. Concentration and substitution bars cleared, but proposer itself concedes "not formally export-controlled" and "allied-aligned supply mitigates near-term risk." This is the structural distinction: CRITICAL requires hostile-jurisdiction policy-risk vector (Dy anchor); EUV blanks are a tail-risk (earthquake, facility fire) rather than weaponization scenario. Calibration-consistent rating is HIGH with explicit Japan-shock monitoring.

## Pre-commercial (1)

### Helium-3
- **Status:** No commercial production at scale. Global supply exclusively a byproduct of tritium decay from nuclear weapons stockpile maintenance.
- **DOE Isotope Program** is dominant Western supplier; Russian tritium-decay stockpile other meaningful source. Annual global supply ~20-30 kg.
- **Allocation-based, not market-priced.** Helion fusion roadmap and lunar regolith proposals (Interlune, Blue Origin) are 2030s+.
- **Pre-commercial** is the framework-correct designation. Boron-10 substituted for many neutron detection applications post-2008 shortage.

### Scandium
- **Rationale for UNRATED:** Public production data is genuinely thin. Global production estimated at only ~25-30 t/yr (USGS 2024) with no transparent spot market. NIOST estimates China ~66%, Russia ~26%, Philippines ~6% but commercial trade is largely opaque bilateral. April 2025 China dual-use list includes Sc, suggesting strategic relevance, but frontier-stack volume is genuinely speculative — physical AI stack demand pull is real (BMW/Airbus piloting Sc-Al) but not yet bottleneck-material at current scale.
- **Confidence: LOW.** Multiple non-Chinese projects (Rio Tinto Sorel-Tracy QC byproduct, Nyrstar Hobart byproduct, NioCorp Elk Creek Nebraska, Sunrise Energy Metals NSW) but commercial volumes uncertain. UNRATED is calibration-honest given thin public data and ambiguous cohort fit.

## Open Questions for Founder Confirmation

The verifier overrode the proposer on 15 of 24 elevated proposals. The following deserve explicit confirmation before the ratings are applied to data — these are the calls where reasonable people could disagree and where the framework's hostile-jurisdiction discipline did most of the work.

### Tier-down calls (CRITICAL → HIGH) — confirm before applying

1. **Neodymium (NdPr oxide):** CRITICAL → HIGH. Hinges on April 2025 MOFCOM list deliberately excluding light REEs (Nd, Pr, La, Ce). If you read NdPr as politically inseparable from Dy/Tb regardless of formal listing, CRITICAL holds. Verifier called HIGH based on Lynas + MP Materials commercially qualified ex-China supply today.

2. **Antimony:** CRITICAL → HIGH. The Nov 10, 2025 suspension of the Dec 2024 prohibition (until Nov 27, 2026), the ~18% price correction Dec 2025-Mar 2026, and the 3,834t shadow re-routing materially abated the crisis. If you weight defense applications (which are largely outside the Robotnik universe scope) more heavily, CRITICAL is defensible. Verifier called HIGH on Robotnik-relevance grounds.

3. **Photoresist (EUV + ArF immersion):** CRITICAL → HIGH. The downgrade rests on Japan being an allied jurisdiction without binding export controls. If you treat ~100% single-country dependency as CRITICAL regardless of alliance status (the JSR/JIC consolidation supports this), CRITICAL is defensible.

4. **Synthetic fused silica / EUV mask blanks:** CRITICAL → HIGH. Same logic as photoresist — concentration and substitution bars cleared (100% Japan, 3-5yr qual), but jurisdictional alliance is the load-bearing distinction. Calibration-consistent rating is HIGH with explicit Japan-shock monitoring. **This is the closest call in the batch.**

### Tier-down calls (HIGH → MEDIUM) — confirm before applying

5. **Lithium:** HIGH → MEDIUM. Driven by Na-ion commercial scale (CATL Naxtra, BYD), ex-China refining at scale, and no export controls. Hydroxide sub-field is more concentrated than carbonate — a sub-field-specific HIGH for hydroxide alone could be defensible.

6. **Cobalt:** HIGH → MEDIUM. LFP at >40% of EV deployment, Indonesian HPAL byproduct, no binding export controls, price collapse. Aerospace/humanoid actuator high-energy-density use case is narrow.

7. **Niobium:** HIGH → MEDIUM. Friendly-jurisdiction concentration (Brazil), Niobec (Quebec) operational secondary, vanadium commercial substitute in HSLA.

8. **Bismuth:** HIGH → MEDIUM. No formal export controls (key distinction from Ga HIGH anchor); AI-stack role peripheral (thermoelectric niche).

9. **Germanium:** HIGH → MEDIUM. Sits at lower edge of HIGH band (~60% China); ex-China qualified supply (Teck, Umicore, Nyrstar) and ~30% recyclate buffer. **Closest call in this tier** — symmetric with Gallium on export-control regime, but materially less concentrated. If you weight policy symmetry with Ga over concentration math, HIGH holds.

10. **Ruthenium:** HIGH → MEDIUM. Johnson Matthey 2025 puts SA at ~71% (not 93% as proposed); EUV absorber claim inverts reality (Ta-based remains incumbent); ~26% PGM recyclate.

11. **Hafnium:** HIGH → MEDIUM. Concentration is in NATO jurisdictions (ATI US + Framatome FR); no hostile export regime. Russia ChepetskyMZ already sanctioned out.

12. **Neon (lithography grade):** HIGH → MEDIUM. Proposer self-flagged as boundary case. No formal controls, active diversification already deployed, EUV bypasses neon entirely. If formal China controls appeared, re-escalate to HIGH immediately.

13. **Xenon:** HIGH → MEDIUM. Production share figures in proposal not credibly substantiated against authoritative sources; Linde Leuna 2023 and Air Liquide Cheonan 2025 expansions.

14. **Tungsten Hexafluoride (WF6):** HIGH → MEDIUM. Production itself is allied-jurisdiction (Resonac, Kanto Denka, SK Materials, Versum, Air Products, Linde); Co commercial at Intel 10nm/Intel 7 and TSMC N5+ since ~2018; Feb 2025 W control is licensing not denial. Upstream W feedstock CRITICAL handled separately under Tungsten entry.

15. **GaN substrates:** HIGH → MEDIUM. The HVPE bulk freestanding sub-segment is genuinely ~80% Japan, but commercial volume power GaN runs on GaN-on-Si epi with multi-country supply (Taiwan, US, EU, China).
