## Summary observations

All 56 commodities place to Tier 1 (Upstream Materials) by default with 7 exceptions at Tier 4 for engineered substrates (SiC, GaN, EUV mask blanks, GOES) and formulated fab consumables (photoresist, CMP slurry, sputtering targets). The boundary cluster sits predominantly at the fab-gas/battery-precursor edge — 19 of 56 entries (34%) flag as boundary cases, with the framework's explicit "chemically converted precursor stays Tier 1" precedent (battery-grade salts, APT) extended consistently to fab gases (WF6, NF3, SiH4, NH3, HF), aerospace propellants (hydrazine, N2O4), and ultra-pure isotopes (He-3, hyperpure polysilicon).
