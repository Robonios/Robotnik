## The two corrections (folded into Draft 8)

**Claim 11 (material).** Draft 7 read: *"…the gallium in NVIDIA's power electronics, the indium in its optics, the rare earths in everything that moves, need Chinese export licences, and the truce covering those materials lapses in November."*
- Indium is **not** in the truce. It is controlled separately (MOFCOM Announcement 10/2025) and does not lapse. Gallium/germanium and the rare-earth expansion are the truce items; the baseline April-2025 rare-earth magnet licences were never suspended.
- Split dates: rare-earth expansion to **10 Nov 2026**; gallium/germanium to **27 Nov 2026** (White House Fact Sheet, 1 Nov 2025; MOFCOM Announcements 70 & 72 of 2025).
- Draft 8: indium struck; *"the truce covering them lapses in November 2026."* Precise dates belong in the policy layer.

**Claim 3 (material).** Draft 7 read: *"…raising $25 billion, its first debt since 2021, to help fund those commitments."*
- The prospectus use of proceeds is **general corporate purposes and refinancing**, not the memory/optical commitments. Press *read* it as build-out financing; the editorial stated it as fact.
- Draft 8: *"…its first bond issuance since 2021, for general corporate purposes and to refinance existing debt."*

## The two nuances

- **Claim 9 (applied).** The $2.3bn line is reported as **Automotive** only; NVIDIA does not break out robotics (it sits in Data Center). Draft 8: *"robotics and vehicles"* → *"automotive"*.
- **Claim 7 (noted, not changed).** The non-hyperscaler ~50% is NVIDIA's "ACIE" bucket (AI-clouds + industrial + enterprise + **sovereign**). *"Governments and enterprises"* undersells it, but the body already names sovereign states and CoreWeave, so this was left as written.

**Watch-note (no edit).** Draft 8 says Broadcom's backlog is *"tens of billions"* — correct. If a number is ever pinned, use **~$73bn** (AI order backlog, Dec 2025); the sweep's ~$45bn is stale.
